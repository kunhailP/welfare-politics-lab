## A14 — Major scientific distinction: the new control is a valid illustration of insufficiency, not a new observed failure

**Evidence/location:** `marginal_control.py::construct_controls`, `tests/test_marginal_control.py`, `protocol.md`, Evaluation-only development addendum; `author_control_receipt.json`. The construction reads human outcomes and uses integer cycles to change group-conditioned allocations while conserving group sums and pooled complete-recipient-profile sums. It explicitly disclaims new-theorem, actual-model and sharp-extremum interpretations. The primary target predates this control in the supplied protocol history, which reduces concern that the target was chosen to fit this demonstration.

**Assessment:** This is a useful software negative control and explanation of why the *specified* mean checks cannot identify the target interaction. Matching complete-profile means is a stronger illustration than a constant matching the grand mean. It provides an explicit, bounded, integer counterexample on a declared design, and can check that the evaluator detects a known discrepancy. That is its defensible scientific role.

It does not show that a generator occupies these directions, how often it does so, how plausible those response tables are, or that existing relationship-sensitive diagnostics would miss them. The controls alter allocation distributions, individual trajectories and group-by-profile conditional outcomes; a diagnostic sensitive to one of those changes may detect them immediately. Being outcome-informed is appropriate for this software purpose, but disqualifies the controls as forecasts, inexpensive replacements for respondents, or evidence of model error.

**Fix/verification criterion:** Keep this as a small illustrative panel or methods/supplement control. Label the preserved objects precisely and report what is not preserved alongside them. Require actual logged generator responses for every empirical error claim. Do not interpret control contrast magnitude as a worst-case bound or frequency of plausible model failure. The author-supplied values around +1,900/−1,752 CAD and human contrast around 55 CAD were not independently reproduced from raw data here and must retain that status.

## A15 — Major empirical gap: the remaining contribution is an estimand-specific audit, and needs a demonstrated consequence

**Evidence/location:** `protocol.md`, Estimands and models, Common-task summaries, and Unresolved collection gates; `critique_questions.md`. There are still no respondent-model outcomes. J_FN and J_FI are continuous recorded-rollout quantities, and broader inference/classification remains disabled.

**Strongest remaining contribution:** Evaluate whether adding a real respondent-income fact helps reproduce a prespecified human-reference allocation moderation contrast, while distinguishing (i) an archive projection driven partly by task composition, (ii) explicit label sensitivity on common tasks, and (iii) uncertainty due to invalid actions. This is narrower than a general validation framework but is coherent and could yield a useful welfare-politics methods case study. It does not identify human class interest, a psychological mechanism, or persistent-agent behaviour.

**Evidence that would materially strengthen it:**

1. Actual isolated N/F/F-I outputs on the frozen matched cohort, scored against exactly the same human X, with signed discrepancies and both loss changes. Report all specified outcomes, including mixed or null results. A negative realized J means closer on that benchmark; it is not automatically meaningful or reliable improvement.
2. Actual common-task and presentation-audit outcomes that establish what the conditioning comparison means under the stated reconstruction. Do not subtract the common-task DID from the archive projection as if this mechanically decomposed it: their tasks, weighting and counterfactual labels differ. Their relation is diagnostic unless a common target/decomposition is explicitly derived.
3. A concrete diagnostic consequence. For example, composition checks change an initially tempting interpretation of a group-blind contrast, or actual invalid-action completion ranges overturn a complete-case claim or demonstrate that it cannot be adjudicated. Synthetic injected failures and oracle controls may verify the analysis but do not constitute this empirical consequence.
4. If claiming stable generator behaviour, execute a frozen repeat plan; if claiming transfer across models, obtain a suitably scoped second-model result. A single recorded rollout can remain an honestly descriptive result without these extensions, but its scientific reach is correspondingly limited. Restore substantive margins only before restoring categorical importance/equivalence claims.

A useful result need not show harmful conditioning. Faithful, improved, mixed or inconclusive outputs are admissible; the project should not require one preferred sign. Conversely, a small descriptive score change with no consequential diagnostic finding would leave the methods increment modest. Further software controls would not repair that limitation. For predictive-advantage claims, the disjoint-human simple comparator remains necessary; the current intervention-only scope can omit it.

## A16 — Minor wording precision: name the preserved summaries without suggesting all main effects are invariant

**Evidence/location:** `marginal_control.py` and `author_control_receipt.json::preserved_exactly` say “all recipient main-effect summaries.”

**Consequence/fix:** Read narrowly, pooled recipient-only mean summaries and corresponding recipient-only regression projections are preserved by complete-profile sum conservation on a fixed design. But in the fully interacted model, a coefficient labelled a recipient “main effect” is a reference-group effect and can change. Use “pooled recipient-only mean contrasts/projections on the fixed design” and keep group-conditioned effects explicitly outside the preserved set. Verification is a clear mapping from each reported preserved metric to the conserved sums; no new modelling machinery is needed.
