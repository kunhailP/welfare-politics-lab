# Nearest-work distinction

This table describes the central targets of the sources reviewed so far. It is
not an exhaustive novelty certification or a claim about every appendix.

| Work | Human reference and target | Main distinction still to demonstrate here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Taday Morocho et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3774905.3795477) ([open version](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18462v1)) | Matched WVS survey items, persona versus no persona, aggregate and subgroup alignment | A specific respondent-by-recipient allocation contrast, explicit-income ablation, common-task diagnostic and failure-weight implications; generic subgroup distortion is already established nearby |
| [FairFund-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28934v2) | Financial-need appeals, audit task/presentation variants, deservingness heuristics; no matched human allocations for those exact appeals | A declared matched human task reference and the fate of a specified group contrast, while treating reconstruction and presentation as validity limits |
| [Ashokkumar et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10742-x) | Predictions across many social-science experimental effects | This one domain cannot support a similarly broad claim; additional value must concern the particular information intervention and a consequential diagnostic |
| [Lee et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05828) | Data-derived personas, population/domain mismatch, data-access regimes | Do not claim richer personas are novel or beneficial by definition; disclose zero-label versus human-trained access and costs |
| [Xie et al., SSDataBench](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2538145123) | Statistical realism including bivariate relations and multivariate prediction, across cross-sectional and longitudinal data | Do not characterize existing evaluation as mean-only; a new audit needs a specific consequential error or diagnostic that adds to these tests |
| [Economic-preference validation across74 countries](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02611-4) | Publisher excerpts describe matched human economic preferences, demographic gradients and persona ablations | Income ablation or subgroup structure alone is insufficient novelty; full-text verification remains required for a precise incremental claim |

The linear completion-bound identity is elementary. A reusable implementation
and adversarial tests are useful research infrastructure, not evidence of a new
statistical theorem. An empirical method contribution would need to show a
substantive inference that conventional aggregate/complete-case checks miss,
or accurately establish where a conditional generator is useful.

The current strongest defensible positioning is a narrow synthetic-survey
component audit in welfare politics. It does not yet establish general population
simulation, causal human motives, a new theory of redistribution or predictive
superiority over a simple human-trained model.
