# Prospective empirical claim and reporting table — version1

Written after human-source analysis and fixed statistical-reference fits,
before any respondent-model generation. This is development after inspecting
human results, not preregistration or an untouched human test. The locked cohort,
primary gamma/J_FN, secondary J_FI and generation hold are unchanged. No new
composite, importance threshold or preferred human subset is introduced.

## One question

**How closely do generated responses reproduce the direction, CAD magnitude
and errors of both household-income groups' recipient-income comparisons,
and how does adding the observed socioeconomic fact bundle change the fixed
moderation score alongside its two group-specific components?**

Income groups operationalize observed household-income bands, not broader class
identity or actual political coalitions. No binary overall fidelity judgement is
specified. The version7 participant-background clarification changes wording
before generation while retaining the locked estimands and reporting table.

The explicit-income question remains secondary F versus F-I. This audits a
component used in welfare-opinion research. It does not measure actual votes,
policy coalitions, individual unanimity, national welfare politics, human
self-interest or solidarity mechanisms. A selected Canadian2020 survey and its
reconstructed tasks cannot establish those objects.

## Interpretations fixed before respondent generation

| Result | Permitted statement | Interpretation to avoid |

| Primary J_FN below zero | The income/job-risk/parenthood bundle reduced absolute moderation error on this fixed cohort/run | Income alone helped; reliable synthetic citizens; meaningful political improvement without a margin |
| Secondary J_FI and signed F-minus-F-I | The explicit-income sentence changed the score/contrast conditional on other facts | All income knowledge removed; causal human-income or stereotype mechanism |
| Both component contrasts and errors | How each group's30k-minus120k comparison aligns, including cancellation and trade-offs | A good difference identifies both levels, or every group member agrees |
| Component direction differs from human reference | A specific adjusted comparison differs on these tasks when its completion bounds exclude numerical zero | New class conflict, real voting patterns or a population sign claim |
| Common-task label audit | Model input responsiveness under equal base-task weights | Human counterfactual evidence or a decomposition obtained by subtracting archive gamma |
| Repeat/presentation/model comparisons | Variation on the predeclared runs/tasks/models | Expected-generator CI or universal robustness |
| Human-trained references | Practical performance under declared labelled-data access and fixed features | Equal-resource superiority or proof future LLMs never saw public source labels |

## Frozen reporting table

Rows include the observed human reference, N, F, F-I, B_mean,
B_recipient_OLS and B_income_interacted_OLS. Statistical references remain
conditional-mean predictors, never respondent outputs or provider logs.

Columns are both group-specific recipient-income contrasts, moderation gamma,
their signed human-reference errors, and J_FN/J_FI plus the signed explicit-
income change where applicable. Report the exact linear completion bounds and
existing conservative nonlinear-loss bounds. The unrestricted fully interacted
design separates outcomes by group, so the continuous component-completion joint
set is rectangular. Keep gamma's direct weights for numerical consistency; do
not generalize this separability to partially interacted or restricted designs.

A companion table retains same-output means/distributions, the stronger
conditional diagnostic and cell sizes, plus invalidity and missing contrast
weight. Report every fixed-reference CV/main RMSE and MAE as prediction
diagnostics with the conditional-mean interpretation. Do not select a metric or
reference for a desired result. Missing LLM values may not silently disappear
from fidelity denominators. No automatic fidelity pass/fail is defined.

A narrow primary-score gain may coexist with component deterioration. Report
both without declaring one global winner. A tiny score difference is not a
consequential political error. Source-cohort sensitivity stays separate from main
scoring; never evaluate the main coefficient against remainder gamma as if X
were unchanged.

The additions improve evaluation credibility, not empirical novelty by
themselves. Benefit, deterioration, mixed and inconclusive results are all
admissible. If only a small known persona effect appears, retain a limited
replication claim or redesign the question. Do not expand baseline/oracle
families until a preferred ordering appears. Native isolation/logging,
model/settings/cap and repeat/presentation execution remain unresolved.
